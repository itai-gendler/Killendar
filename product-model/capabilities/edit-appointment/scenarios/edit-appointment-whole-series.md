# Shift a series from a later occurrence

## Trigger

The User opens a later date generated by a repeating series, chooses the whole series, and changes its time, day, details or repeat pattern.

## Outcome

The series keeps its own first date as the anchor, moved by the same amount the User moved the date they opened, rather than restarting from that date. A shift by whole days moves the skipped dates with it, so each deleted date stays deleted.
