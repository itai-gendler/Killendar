# Delete one generated date

## Trigger

The User opens a date generated by a repeating series, keeps just this date, chooses Delete and confirms.

## Outcome

The date is added to the series' skipped dates and disappears; the rest of the series stays. In a series that ends after a number of times, the deleted date still counts, so no date is added at the end.
